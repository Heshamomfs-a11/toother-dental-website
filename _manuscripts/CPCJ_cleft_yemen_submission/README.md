# CPCJ submission package: Unoperated cleft lip and palate in Yemen (qualitative study)

Submission files for *The Cleft Palate-Craniofacial Journal* (CPCJ, SAGE / American Cleft Palate-Craniofacial Association), prepared for the ScholarOne portal.

> **Privacy warning.** This GitHub repository is **public** and has GitHub Pages enabled. The folder name starts with an underscore so a Jekyll-based Pages build will not publish it, but anyone who browses the repository or this branch can download these files, including the title page with your phone number. Download what you need, then consider making the repository private or deleting this branch. Do not merge this folder into `main` unless you are comfortable with that exposure.

## 2. Corrections made to the draft package

1. **Editor-in-Chief.** The cover letter was addressed to Dr. Jeffrey Hammoudeh. The journal's current Editor-in-Chief is **Dr. Jamie Perry, PhD, CCC-SLP** (East Carolina University, Greenville, NC). The letter now opens "Dear Dr. Perry".
2. **Journal name.** Standardized to *The Cleft Palate-Craniofacial Journal* (hyphenated) throughout.
3. **Single-author consistency.** "The author(s)" became "The author"; "on behalf of the research team" and "we" were removed or made passive. If co-authors are added, see section 3.
4. **Statements and Declarations.** Rebuilt to the SAGE heading set: Ethical considerations, Consent to participate, Consent for publication, Author contributions, Declaration of conflicting interests, Funding, Data availability statement, Trial registration (Not applicable), Acknowledgements. SAGE requires the **full name of the ethics committee and the approval number**; the original wording ("institutional ethics committee at the Faculty of Dentistry, Ibb University / participating surgical outreach centers") would be returned for correction, so it is now a placeholder.
5. **Abstract length.** The journal limit for Original Articles is **250 words**, structured under eight fixed headings (Objective, Design, Setting, Patients/Participants, Interventions, Main Outcome Measure(s), Results, Conclusions). Your stated count is 258, so the abstract must be trimmed. The template contains a compliant draft built only from the study summary you supplied (249 words including headings) for you to reconcile with your own abstract.
6. **COREQ checklist.** Restored the official 32 guide questions and sub-domain groupings. Item 23 now states plainly that transcripts were *not* returned and describes the oral member-checking instead; item 13 asks for the number of refusals, which the draft did not report.
7. **Braun and Clarke citation.** The draft cited "reflexive thematic analysis (Braun & Clarke, 2006)". The 2006 paper describes thematic analysis; "reflexive" thematic analysis is the terminology of Braun and Clarke's 2019 paper (*Qualitative Research in Sport, Exercise and Health*) and their 2021 book. Either cite one of those alongside 2006, or describe the method as "thematic analysis following Braun and Clarke's six-phase framework". The cover letter no longer carries the inline year.
8. **Cover letter additions** required or expected by the journal: statement that the main document is anonymized, list of files uploaded, originality and no-concurrent-submission statement, ethics and consent statement, trial registration not applicable, no conflicts, no funding.

## 4. Journal requirements this package was built against

Verified in October 2026 through the journal's SAGE author-instructions page and the SAGE journal home page (direct fetching was blocked in this environment, so figures were confirmed through search snippets of those pages):

- Double-anonymized peer review: separate title page carrying authors, affiliations, corresponding author, and Statements and Declarations; anonymized main document; no author names in file names, headers or footers; no details identifying authors, institutions, funders or approval committees in reviewed files.
- Original Article: structured abstract of up to 250 words with the headings Objective, Design, Setting, Patients/Participants, Interventions, Main Outcome Measure(s), Results, Conclusions; at least 3 keywords; body of about 7,000 words excluding abstract, references and captions; up to 6 tables and figures combined.
- AMA reference style for all submissions since 1 January 2022.
- Ethics statement under its own "Ethical considerations" heading, naming the committee in full with the approval number; separate consent statements; data availability statement; declaration of conflicting interests and funding statements in SAGE wording.
- Reporting guidelines (EQUATOR Network) followed; the COREQ checklist is the appropriate one for interview-based qualitative research.
- Editor-in-Chief: Jamie Perry, PhD, CCC-SLP.

Sources: https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/cpc ; https://journals.sagepub.com/home/cpc ; https://acpacares.org/cpcj-author-guidelines-update/
